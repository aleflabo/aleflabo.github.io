My work focuses on translating breakthrough AI technologies into scalable enterprise solutions.

As CTO at Procederai, I'm spearheading the development of AI systems that automate complex procedural tasks, leveraging my research expertise in:

- **Computer Vision** - Enabling machines to interpret and act on visual information
- **Video Analysis** - Real-time understanding of dynamic visual content
- **Egocentric Vision** - First-person perspective AI for human-centric applications
